# Kwerft trademark policy

**Draft, pending legal review.** "Kwerft" is a trademark of Enzo Hilzinger;
a registration is being applied for. The license (AGPL-3.0-only, see
[LICENSE](LICENSE)) covers the code. It does not grant rights to the name
or the logo: this policy does.

The point of the policy is simple: when someone sees "Kwerft", they should
get the software from this project, or something the project stands behind.
It should never get in the way of using, changing or sharing the code.

## What you may do without asking

- **Talk about Kwerft.** Write articles, give talks, make videos, compare it
  with other tools, or teach it, using the name "Kwerft" to refer to this
  project.
- **Say what works with it.** "Runs on Kwerft", "for Kwerft", "compatible
  with Kwerft", "a Kwerft template" — as long as it is true and does not
  suggest the project made or endorses your product.
- **Redistribute unmodified releases** under their own name, for example in
  a package repository or a server image, provided you keep the license and
  copyright notices.
- **Use the name in your own community.** Meetups, user groups and chat
  channels may use "Kwerft" in their name if it is clear they are run by
  users, for example "Kwerft users Berlin".
- **Run Kwerft for yourself or your organisation**, including for your own
  customers' workloads, and say that you do.

## What needs permission

- **Naming a modified version "Kwerft".** You may publish changed versions
  of the code (the AGPL allows it), but under another name. You may say they
  are "based on Kwerft" or "a fork of Kwerft".
- **Offering Kwerft as a hosted service under the name.** A service may say
  that it runs Kwerft, but may not be called Kwerft or "Kwerft Cloud", or
  look like it is offered by the project.
- **Products, company names and domain names** that contain "Kwerft" or
  something easily confused with it (like "Kwerft Hosting", kwerft-pro.com).
- **Merchandise** with the name or logo for sale.
- **The logo**, other than unchanged to point to this project.

Ask through an issue at https://github.com/ehilzinger/kwerft/issues or the
contact on https://github.com/ehilzinger; permission is usually easy to get
for things that help users.

## How to write it

- "Kwerft", with a capital K. Not "KWerft" or "kwerft" (except in code,
  commands and URLs, where the name is lower case).
- When you use the name prominently, add: "Kwerft is a trademark of Enzo
  Hilzinger."
- Do not change the logo's shape, colours or proportions, or combine it
  with other marks.
