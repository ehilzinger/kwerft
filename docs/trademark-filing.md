# Filing the "Kwerft" trademark — checklist

For the owner (Enzo Hilzinger); written 2026-10-07. Not legal advice: a
trademark lawyer can do the search and the filing for a fixed fee, and
should review [TRADEMARKS.md](../TRADEMARKS.md) and [CLA.md](../CLA.md) at
the same time.

## Recommendation

An **EU trade mark** (EUTM) at the EUIPO, as a **word mark "KWERFT"**, in
**classes 9 and 42**: one application covers all EU member states, including
Germany, for 10 years.

| | EU trade mark (EUIPO) | German trade mark (DPMA) |
|---|---|---|
| Covers | all EU member states | Germany only |
| Official fee, filed online | €850 for one class, +€50 for the second, +€150 for each further class | €290 for up to three classes, +€100 for each further class |
| Classes 9 + 42 | **€900** | **€290** |
| Valid for | 10 years, renewable | 10 years, renewable |

The German mark is the cheap start if Kwerft's users stay mostly in Germany
for now; an EU mark can be filed later, and within six months of the German
filing it can claim its filing date (Paris Convention priority). For an
open-source product used across Europe from the start, the EU mark is the
better fit.

## Classes and wording

Use the pre-approved terms from TMclass (https://euipo.europa.eu/ec2/) so
the application is not held up by the examiner. Candidates:

- **Class 9:** downloadable computer software; downloadable software for
  managing, deploying and monitoring software applications on servers and
  computer clusters; downloadable cloud computing software.
- **Class 42:** software as a service [SaaS]; platform as a service [PaaS];
  hosting of computer software applications for others; installation,
  maintenance and updating of computer software; IT consultancy.

Class 35 (business services) or 41 (training) only if Kwerft will sell
those under the name.

## Steps

1. **Clearance search** before paying: TMview (https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/)
   for identical and similar marks ("Kwerft", "Werft", "Qwerft", …) in
   classes 9 and 42 in the EU and Germany. A lawyer's search is more
   thorough; earlier marks can oppose for three months after publication.
2. **Applicant:** you personally, or a company if you found one for Kwerft
   first. A registered mark can be transferred to a company later (a small
   fee), which CLA.md section 8 also allows for the CLA.
3. **File online:** EUIPO e-filing (https://euipo.europa.eu/ — "Apply for
   a trade mark") or DPMAdirektWeb (https://direkt.dpma.de/marke/).
   Word mark, the classes and terms above, pay the fee.
4. **Opposition period:** three months after publication (EU) or after
   registration (Germany).
5. **Afterwards:** "®" only once registered; until then "Kwerft" or
   "Kwerft™". Remove "a registration is being applied for" from
   TRADEMARKS.md, add the registration number, and update docs/plan.md.
6. **Logo:** a separate figurative mark, once the logo is final.
